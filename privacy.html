Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

BookTalkieTees is a book-inspired design browsing app and website. BookTalkieTees is the controller of the personal information described in this policy. The app and website do not require an account. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Information and purposes

We do not collect signup details, shipping addresses, billing details, or payment information inside the app or website. Favorites, cart items, marketplace preference, consent preference, and cached catalog data are stored locally on your browser or app device to provide the features you request.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use it to respond, provide support, handle requests, protect the service, and maintain necessary business records.

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical request information such as IP address, request time, requested file, browser information, and security logs to deliver and protect the site. The app and website also load catalog files and images from BookTalkieTees web resources, which may be cached on your device.

Legal bases

Where GDPR or similar law applies, optional Analytics is processed only with your consent. Support communications are processed to respond to your request, take steps you ask us to take, and for our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the service. Essential local storage and technical delivery logs are used as necessary to provide, secure, and maintain the website and app. You are not legally required to provide support information, but we may be unable to respond without the information needed to understand your request.

Optional Website Analytics

With your permission, the BookTalkieTees website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate website usage, such as pages or sections viewed, marketplace selections, product types viewed, and clicks that lead to Amazon. We do not send names, email addresses, support messages, search text, favorites, or cart contents to Google Analytics.

When Analytics is enabled, your browser sends information to Google that can include the page URL, device and browser information, interaction events, approximate location derived from an IP address, and cookie identifiers. Google states that GA4 uses IP addresses at collection time and discards them before they are logged. Google may process information in the United States and other countries. BookTalkieTees disables advertising storage, advertising personalization, and Google Signals in its website tag.

Analytics is optional worldwide. The Google Analytics tag is not loaded unless you select “Accept analytics.” If you reject or withdraw consent, Analytics events stop and BookTalkieTees removes accessible Google Analytics cookies from this site. You can change your choice at any time using . Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. We also treat an enabled Global Privacy Control signal as a rejection of Analytics.

Google provides more information about how it uses data from sites that use its services in its partner sites notice.

The mobile app retrieves catalog files and images from BookTalkieTees web resources, but the website’s Google Analytics tag does not run in the native mobile app. BookTalkieTees does not currently use a mobile analytics SDK. Normal server or hosting logs may record technical request information when devices retrieve website resources.

Third-Party Services

Service providers and recipients may include Google Analytics when you consent, GitHub and its infrastructure providers for website hosting, and the provider that processes email you send us. These providers process information under their own terms and applicable data-protection commitments.

BookTalkieTees links to Amazon marketplaces. We do not receive affiliate commissions from these links. Clicking a link takes you to Amazon, where Amazon independently handles its website, account, payment, delivery, returns, and transaction information under its own terms and privacy policy. Suggested prices on BookTalkieTees are estimates; the price, availability, taxes, delivery charges, and terms displayed by Amazon control.

International processing

Where applicable law requires transfer safeguards, processing is governed by the provider’s data-protection terms and recognized safeguards such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses. Contact us if you want information about safeguards relevant to your request.

Your Choices and Rights

You may reject or withdraw optional Analytics consent at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection; to withdraw consent; and to appeal or complain to your local data-protection authority. We do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects using automated processing. Contact us to exercise a right. We may need enough information to verify and respond to the request, and we will respond as required by applicable law without discriminating against you for exercising a privacy right.

Retention and Deletion

Local favorites, cart items, marketplace choices, consent choices, and cached catalog files remain on your device until you remove them, clear browser or app data, or uninstall the app. Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain necessary business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations, after which they are deleted or anonymized. When website Analytics is enabled, BookTalkieTees configures GA4 user-level and event-level retention for up to two months; GA cookies can remain longer as described above unless consent is withdrawn or cookies are cleared. Hosting and email providers retain technical records under their own retention schedules. You may contact us to request deletion of personal information we control.

Children

BookTalkieTees is a general-audience catalog intended for people able to make or supervise marketplace purchases. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A “Kids” category describes product themes and is not intended to solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child supplied personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.
